Import from CSV

Adds the employees, projects, and monthly revenue found in the file. Employees and projects that already exist are matched by name, not duplicated.

Which columns can I use?

A header row is required. Employee and Project are the only mandatory columns. Revenue can be given either way:

  • One row per month — add Year, Month and Amount.
  • One column per month — name the columns Jan 2026, 2026-02, … (or use plain Jan…Dec plus a Year column).

Optional: Client, MRR, Active, and the bonus dial per employee — Min, Max, Threshold, Bonus Rate, Hire Date. Amounts may include $ and thousands separators. Blank amounts are skipped; 0 is stored as zero.

Share a read-only report

Anyone with the link can read 's bonus report for the year you choose — the dial, the pace, and the month and project breakdown. They cannot sign in, change anything, or see your other employees. Revenue stays live, so the report reflects later edits. Revoke a link whenever you like.
